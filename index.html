How It Works

Turn Daily Movement into Visual Progress

FitnRizz is designed to help you stay physically active by turning outdoor walks and runs into an interactive visual journey.

1. Walk & Track Outdoors

Step outside for your daily routine or walk. FitnRizz records your steps and outdoor distance continuously using low-power GPS.

2. See Territory Coverage

Your walking routes map out visual progress zones across your area, giving you immediate feedback on the distance you've conquered.

3. Maintain Consistency

Keep your territory progress active by staying regular with daily walks, climbing leaderboards, and building long-term fitness habits.

Core Features

Built for Motivation & Habit Consistency

Key features engineered into the FitnRizz Android application to support your daily wellness journey.

Map-Based Progress Tracking

High-accuracy GPS maps show visual territory zones covered over time as you complete outdoor walking and running sessions.

Activity & Step Logging

Track daily step counts, walking distance, estimated calorie expenditure, and activity milestones to monitor your physical consistency.

Leaderboards & Rankings

Optional community leaderboards that showcase step consistency and territory coverage to add friendly motivation.

Battery-Friendly Background GPS

Optimized background location tracking captures continuous walks while conserving device battery life.

Secure Firebase Cloud Storage

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the FitnRizz Android app, GPS location usage, and data management.

FitnRizz is a wellness and fitness application that tracks physical activity (such as steps and distance walked) and uses gamified features like map-based territory progress and leaderboards to help motivate consistency and long-term healthy habits.

FitnRizz is currently available for Android on Google Play.

FitnRizz uses your device GPS while the app is active (or in background if enabled) to record walking distance and power the map-based territory visualization features.

You can request account deletion in the app via Settings > Delete Account. A 15-day grace period is provided: if you log back in during those 15 days, the deletion is cancelled. If you do not log in, your account and data are permanently deleted at the end of the 15-day period.
